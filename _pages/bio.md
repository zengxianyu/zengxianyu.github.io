Dr. Yu Zeng' research studies visual imagination: how machines can predict, generate, and understand visual change by looking for shared structure across tasks and modalities and building methods that transfer and scale. Her current work spans computer vision, world models for embodied AI, and visual synthesis. 
She received her PhD from Johns Hopkins University and currently works at Toyota Research Institute as a researcher. Prior to this, she also worked for NVIDIA and Lightspeed Studios as a researcher and interned at Adobe Research.
